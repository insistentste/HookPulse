## 📖 What is HookPulse?

Every YouTube Shorts creator knows the pain. You upload a video, it gets a few likes, and then... it flatlines at exactly **1,000 views**. 

Why? Because of the **Seed Audience**. YouTube shows your video to a tiny test group first. If they swipe away in the first 3 seconds, your video is dead on arrival. 

**HookPulse** is a local, offline Windows desktop tool that tells you if your Short will pass the Seed Test *before* you hit publish. It analyzes your first 3 seconds—visuals, pacing, audio, and on-screen text—and gives you a definitive score with actionable advice. 

No logins. No API keys. No subscription fees. Just you and the algorithm.

---

## ✨ Why You Need This

- 🎯 **The "1,000 View" Predictor:** Get a score from 1 to 10 on how likely your Short is to break the seed audience threshold.
- 🧠 **AI-Powered Hook Analysis:** Scans your video frame-by-frame and audio waveform to measure retention potential.
- 🔊 **Audio & Visual Sync:** Checks if your text hooks match the visual changes and the background audio.
- 🛡️ **100% Private:** All processing happens locally on your computer. Your videos never leave your machine.
- 💡 **Actionable Feedback:** Tells you exactly *why* a hook fails (e.g., "Too much text on screen in the first 0.5s" or "Audio spike missing").

## 🎬 How to Use

1. **Open HookPulse.**
2. **Drag & Drop** your exported Shorts video (`.mp4`, `.mov`) into the app.
3. **Wait 5 seconds.** The app scans the first 3 seconds of your video.
4. **Read the Report:**
   - 🟢 **Score 8-10:** Your hook is solid. Post it.
   - 🟡 **Score 5-7:** Good, but needs a tweak (check the tips).
   - 🔴 **Score 1-4:** Do not post. It will die at 1,000 views.

## 🧠 Under the Hood

HookPulse uses OpenCV and Librosa to break down your first 3 seconds:

- **Visual Velocity:** Measures frame changes and zoom speed.
- **Audio Dynamics:** Detects volume spikes and silence gaps.
- **Text Density:** Reads on-screen text and checks for cognitive overload.
- **Facial Detection:** Checks if a face appears in the first 3 seconds (the "face effect").

All models are pre-trained and bundled inside the app. You don't need Python or any setup.
